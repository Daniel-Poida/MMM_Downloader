- [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org/) via `imageio-ffmpeg` — LGPL/GPL depending on the binary build.
- [Deno](https://github.com/denoland/deno) — MIT License.
- [uv](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv) — Apache-2.0 OR MIT.
- [Qt for Python / PySide6](https://doc.qt.io/qtforpython-6/) — LGPLv3/GPLv3 or commercial license.
